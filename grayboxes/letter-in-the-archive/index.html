The primary source

What Harriet actually wrote

This is a human-checked transcription of the scan (fictional). Read all of it. The last line matters.

Page 1 · Ash Creek, Kansas, April 9th, 1893 Dear Sister, the creek rose in the night and carried off the footbridge, so the children could not reach the schoolhouse until Thursday. Mr. Ostrander has promised lumber for a new bridge, though I shall believe it when I see it. Page 2 We held lessons in the Lindqvist kitchen, eleven scholars in all, with slates on the flour barrel. Page 3 I have not been paid since January and am living on credit at Hale’s store. Mother’s rheumatism is no better. Your loving sister, Harriet. Page 4 · postscript P.S. The board voted Tuesday to pay me in full by the first of May.

Collection: Ash Creek Historical Society · Item ACHS-1893-014 · 4 pages · Rights: public domain, per the Society
