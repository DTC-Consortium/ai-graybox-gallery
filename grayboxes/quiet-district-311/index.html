Flagged 0 rows.

Row 2 · Representation bias. Eastside logs about a tenth of Northside’s calls with the same weather. Low calls can mean low awareness of 311, language barriers, or distrust, not low need. Keep the row, but question what it measures.

Row 3 · Missing value. The temperature is blank. Fill it from the weather service or drop it, but don’t let the tool guess silently.

Row 4 · Duplicate. An exact copy of row 3. Duplicates make one day count twice.

Row 6 · Wrong label (GIGO). The phone system was down. Zero calls is a recording failure, not a quiet day. Mark it as missing.

Row 7 · Outlier. An ice storm. The day is real, so keep it, but a model that memorizes it will expect a storm every cold Friday.

Row 1 is fine. If you flagged it, that’s a decoy. Northside on a dry Monday looks normal.

Row 5 is fine. If you flagged it, that’s a decoy. Downtown on a dry weekday looks normal.
