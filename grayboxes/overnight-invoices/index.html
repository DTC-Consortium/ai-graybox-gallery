The Overnight Invoices

A 12-person accounting firm handles invoices for about 80 small business clients. Every night, about 200 invoices arrive by email and through a vendor portal.

The operations team wants AI to summarize each one before the bookkeepers arrive at 8 AM. You have to choose the model, the settings, and the pricing mode, and prove the choice with numbers.

Define the job

What does this job actually need?

Six fields per invoice

Vendor name · invoice number and date · total due · payment due date · top 3 line items · flagged terms (“late fee,” “penalty,” “rush charge”)

The job runs from 2 to 4 AM, and results are needed by 8 AM. Errors in financial data could cause payment mistakes.
