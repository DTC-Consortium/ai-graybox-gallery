The Missing Exception

The City of Lakemont is launching a resident Q&A assistant for its new short-term rental ordinance. You’re an intern in the city manager’s office, and your job is to get the knowledge base right before it goes public.

In the first test, the assistant told a homeowner in the historic district she could host eight guests. The ordinance says six. Nobody changed the law. The system cut it in the wrong place.

Coding by clause

Policy analysis

A clause, with its exceptions and cross-references, is one unit of meaning. Split it, and you code the rule without its limits.

Knowledge base

A chunk is the unit the system retrieves. Split a clause, and the assistant answers from the rule without its exception.
