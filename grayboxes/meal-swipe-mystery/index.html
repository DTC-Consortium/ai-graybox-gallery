The prompt changelog

Your grade is how you handle failure

Failed: hallucination

The AI invented a study with a statistic.

Prompt fix: Only use the 30 uploaded sources. Quote the page or section for every claim. If it isn’t there, say so.

Failed: contextual blindness

It coded a schedule conflict as taste, and read “dying for a swipe” literally.

Prompt fix: Here is a glossary of campus terms. List any response you are unsure about instead of guessing.

Failed: bias and runaway loops

Small groups were lumped into “Other,” and the sorting never stopped.

Prompt fix: Make two passes only. Create a separate theme for minority experiences. Stop after pass two.
