The Phantom Sighting

Your field ecology class has 12 trail cameras at the Cedar Bend preserve and 2,000 photos from the fall. An image model just flagged a plains spotted skunk, which would be the preserve’s first record of the species in 30 years.

The preserve director wants to tell the state wildlife agency today. Before anyone does, your class has to decide how much to trust that “yes.”
